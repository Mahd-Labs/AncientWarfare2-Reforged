# Credits - Ancient Warfare 2: Reforged

Ancient Warfare 2: Reforged is an **unofficial** port of the Minecraft mod Ancient Warfare 2 to Minecraft 1.21.1 /
NeoForge 21.1. It is a modified version. It is not made, endorsed, supported or reviewed by the authors of Ancient
Warfare 2 or by anyone named below, and they are not responsible for it or for any problem it causes. Please report
problems with this port to <https://github.com/Mahd-Labs/AncientWarfare2-Reforged/issues>, not to the original authors.

Everything good in this mod comes from the people below. The port only carries their work to a newer Minecraft.

## Original mod

- **Shadowmage** (John Cummens, GitHub shadowmage45) - created Ancient Warfare and Ancient Warfare 2 (2012-2015):
  the design and most of the original code and art.
- **P3pp3rF1y** - maintained and extended Ancient Warfare 2 for Minecraft 1.10-1.12.2 (2017-2021): the 1.12 code base
  this port is made from, the structure, faction and town systems in their 1.12 form.
- **GotoLink** - Ancient Warfare 2 developer (2015-2016). About 11,500 of his lines survive in the 1.12 code base
  (`git blame` of P3pp3rF1y/AncientWarfare2 at 446b7db), in 53 classes this port carries over in ported form, among
  them TownTemplate, TownBoundingArea, the advanced spawner GUIs, GuiResearchBook, NpcNavigator, TileWorksiteBounded,
  the trade classes, SongPlayData and WorldGenTickHandler. He also gave permission for the walled-town walls that
  Mohawky first built for Formivore's walled city generator.
- **Code contributors to the 1.12 repository** (commit authors): WinDanesz, CosmicDan, ThatPolishKid99,
  InfinityRaider, Wade Wegner, mcBegins2Snow, tartaric_acid, syamojin, Phorcis, Mazdallier, HEYMANCXN, Fro-Z, and the
  translators of the language files.
- Original repository: https://github.com/P3pp3rF1y/AncientWarfare2 (branch 1.12.x, commit 446b7db, GPL-3.0-or-later).

  The excerpts are shortened from the originals.
- `sounds/structure/structures/wizard_tower_6.ogg`: "Strange Days" by **Mike Koenig**, from SoundBible.com
  (https://soundbible.com/1741-Strange-Days.html), licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  Converted to Ogg Vorbis (and possibly shortened) for Ancient Warfare 2; shipped unchanged from Ancient Warfare 2.
  This file keeps its own licence (CC BY 3.0), not the GPL.
- The other sounds come from the original mod. **Their sources were not recorded** by the original project, and this
  port could not establish them. If you hold the rights to any of them, open an issue at <https://github.com/Mahd-Labs/AncientWarfare2-Reforged/issues> and the
  sound will be credited or removed.
- Sound files whose own metadata named a commercial library, a film, TV programme or game, or another third party were
  removed from this port, together with untagged files from the same sets (see `tools/licensing_dropped_sounds.txt` in
  the source). Some of the affected NPC sounds now play Minecraft's own sound events instead (no Minecraft audio is
  shipped with this mod); the others are silent.
